Now

Updated August 1, 2026, from my laptop in the night while reading the book “You can just do things” by Jay Yang.

Current Focus

My son is now 11 months old, and I’m trying to make the most of this phase of life with him. These months feel fleeting, and I want to be as present as possible while he’s still this small.

PSFutureMail, a small project that allows people to write letters to their future selves and capture their thoughts for years to come has got around 250 sign-ups so far. And a lot of letters have already been delivered on time. So far, the feedback has been incredibly positive.

I am working on learning react native to build mobile apps for the last few days. This is a fascinating area, and I haven’t got much experience around it yet.

Current Projects

I continue to work full-time as a Team Lead in the Data Platform team at Sophos. Currently, I am building a mobile app called “Snaclet” that asks users to select a few topics and then tell them a lot of facts on that topic. 1 or 2 liners which they can keep swiping like tinder, except here you swipe up or down, instead of left or right.

Outside of work, most of my time is spent being with my son. He brings a lot of smile to my face.

Current Interests
